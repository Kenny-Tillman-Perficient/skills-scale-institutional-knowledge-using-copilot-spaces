# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the system design direction, technical standards, and integration patterns that keep implementation consistent and scalable.

### Responsibilities
- Establish architectural principles and design guardrails
- Review solution designs and technical trade-offs
- Define integration patterns, APIs, and platform standards
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and long-term constraints
- Partner with engineering leads on modernization and resilience decisions

### Goals
- Maintain a coherent, maintainable technical foundation
- Reduce unnecessary rework and integration complexity
- Support scalability, security, and operational stability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical standards documentation and decision records
- Cross-team planning for dependencies and platform impacts

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers align team delivery priorities, staffing, and operational execution so engineering work stays focused and sustainable.

### Responsibilities
- Help plan team capacity, priorities, and work allocation
- Support team health, delivery rhythm, and execution quality
- Remove blockers and coordinate dependencies across teams
- Partner with Product and Project leads on roadmap trade-offs
- Support hiring, onboarding, and engineering team growth

### Goals
- Increase delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Balance speed, quality, and sustainability
- Maintain healthy, high-performing engineering teams

### Typical Communication
- Team planning and delivery check-ins
- Capacity and dependency discussions with PMs and Product leaders
- Operational updates during planning, execution, and release cycles

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure products and delivery activities meet required controls, risk standards, and regulatory expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate security and compliance risk during design and delivery
- Define required controls, reviews, and approval checkpoints
- Coordinate vulnerability remediation and incident response readiness
- Validate relevant privacy, data protection, and governance requirements
- Work with engineering and stakeholders on mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Reduce product and operational risk
- Maintain compliance with policies and regulatory obligations
- Embed security practices into the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and compliance checkpoints
- Risk assessments and remediation planning
- Escalation and incident coordination with leadership and stakeholders

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Leads translate customer feedback, issues, and usage patterns into delivery priorities and operational improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Capture recurring customer pain points and feature requests
- Share product feedback and support trends with delivery teams
- Help prioritize fixes and improvements with customer impact
- Coordinate support readiness for launches and changes
- Contribute to success metrics and customer-facing communications

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Ensure supportability and operational readiness
- Connect customer signals to product and delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Feedback reviews and customer issue triage
- Product prioritization discussions with PM and engineering leads
- Release readiness and support planning conversations

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams measure product outcomes, validate decisions with evidence, and monitor adoption, quality, and business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics, instrumentation, and reporting needs
- Monitor adoption, performance, and business outcomes
- Support experimentation and validation of product decisions
- Identify gaps in data quality, observability, and reporting
- Partner with PM and stakeholders on success measurement

### Goals
- Improve decision quality with measurable evidence
- Detect issues early through monitoring and analysis
- Support continuous optimization of customer and business value

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews and dashboards
- Stakeholder updates on performance and adoption
- Analysis of release impact and user behavior trends

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These role definitions help teams clarify accountability, improve cross-functional collaboration, and support smoother handoffs across planning, execution, and release activities.
